Hall effects
------------

Hall responses at any Fermi level, from the Berry curvature of the bands:
the anomalous Hall conductivity of the Haldane model (its quantized
plateau in the gap, and its non-quantized value in the bands), the same
plateau surviving disorder in a real-space Kubo-Bastin (kernel polynomial)
calculation on a torus, and the intrinsic spin Hall conductivity of the
Kane-Mele model with its departure from quantization under Rashba coupling.
