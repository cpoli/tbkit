Disorder
--------

Anderson localization: the Inverse Participation Ratio vs. disorder
strength, and an extended state shown side by side with a localized one.
The IPR's scaling with size and the multifractal states of a critical
chain, the self-dual Aubry-André transition of a quasiperiodic chain,
Lifshitz tails of the density of states, and the zero modes bound by
vacancies in graphene.
