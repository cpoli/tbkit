Correlations
------------

Interacting electrons, in the Hartree-Fock mean-field approximation of
the Hubbard model: the magnetism of graphene's zigzag edges and of
triangular nanoislands, Lieb's theorem and the ferrimagnet of the Lieb
lattice, and flat-band ferromagnetism (Nagaoka, Mielke, Tasaki). The
non-collinear mean field finds the 120-degree order of the frustrated
triangular lattice.
