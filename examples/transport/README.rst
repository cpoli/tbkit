Transport
---------

Quantum transport through a finite device attached to semi-infinite
leads: Landauer's formula, in which the conductance is a transmission
probability, and the quantized conductance steps of a quantum point
contact. Leads attached automatically from their geometry, narrow
contacts on a wide cavity.

Multi-terminal devices: Landauer-Buttiker conductance matrices and the
quantized resistances of a Hall bar with its edge channels; bond
currents; shot noise and the Fano factor; and the recursive Green's
function for long wires. The scattering matrix from the exact modes of
the leads, the scattering states and the local density of states they
carry. Thermoelectric transport: the Mott formula and the
Wiedemann-Franz law, and their breakdown at a resonance.
