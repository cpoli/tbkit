Topology
--------

Topological band theory: the SSH model's bulk-boundary correspondence
and its Zak phase, the Haldane model's Chern-number phase transition and
Berry curvature, the TKNN quantized Hall conductance of the Hofstadter
bands, zigzag graphene and Kane-Mele helical edge states cut from
ribbons, the Kane-Mele Z2 invariant from Wannier-centre flow and the
Fu-Kane parity criterion, the surface Dirac cone of a 3D topological
insulator (surface spectral function of a semi-infinite crystal), the Thouless quantum pump's exactly quantized
charge transport, a topological (Chern) flat band on the kagome lattice,
the quantum metric of a Bloch band, the local Chern marker of a
disordered flake, the Bott index of a disordered torus, the
entanglement spectrum of a cut SSH ring and Haldane cylinder, the spin
Chern number of the Kane-Mele model with Rashba coupling, the mirror
Chern number of a topological crystalline insulator, the four Z2 indices
of strong and weak 3D topological insulators, the axion angle pumped by
2 pi around a cycle of a lattice Dirac model, the tenfold-way
classification of tight-binding models by their symmetries, and the
diabolical points (conical intersections with a Berry phase of pi) of a
two-parameter family.

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="An insulator responds to an electric field with a magnetization and to a magnetic field with a polarization. Part of that response is a single number \theta, entering the electromagnetic action as the axion term of Wilczek (1987), \frac{\theta e^2}{2\pi h}\int\mathbf{E}\cdot\mathbf{B}: the polarization \mathbf{P} = \frac{\theta}{2\pi}\frac{e^2}{h}\mathbf{B}. Qi, Hughes and Zhang (2008) and Essin, Moore and Vanderbilt (2009) showed that for Bloch electrons \theta is the Chern-Simons integral of the Berry connection of the occupied bands. It is defined modulo 2\pi (a surface can always gain a quantum Hall layer), and time reversal or inversion force \theta = 0 or \pi: \pi is the strong 3D topological insulator.">

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="The kagome lattice&#x27;s flat band (see /api/gallery/flat_bands/plot_flat_bands) isn&#x27;t isolated: at zero field it touches the middle dispersive band exactly at \Gamma (a symmetry-protected degeneracy, both at E=-2t).
