Large lattices
--------------

Lattices far beyond dense diagonalization: sparse neighbour search,
shift-invert eigensolvers, every eigenstate in an energy window by
spectrum slicing, and the kernel polynomial method, which turns spectral
densities, spectral functions, DC, optical and second-order optical
conductivities of hundreds of thousands of sites into sparse
matrix-vector products, and evolves wavepackets in time by the same
Chebyshev recursion. In k-space,
fine meshes are diagonalized in batches, optionally across threads.
