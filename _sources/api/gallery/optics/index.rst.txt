Optics
------

Linear and nonlinear optical response of Bloch bands: the Kubo-Greenwood
optical conductivity and the universal absorption of graphene, the Berry
curvature dipole behind the nonlinear Hall effect, and the shift current
of the bulk photovoltaic effect.
