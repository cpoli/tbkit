Orbitals
--------

Beyond one orbital per site: Slater-Koster bond integrals between s, p
and d orbitals, non-orthogonal bases and their Löwdin orthogonalization,
spin with Rashba spin-orbit coupling and a Zeeman field, and the band
structures they give.

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Atomic orbitals on neighbouring atoms overlap: s = \langle\phi_i|\phi_j\rangle \neq 0. In such a basis the Schrödinger equation is the generalized eigenproblem H\mathbf{c} = ES\mathbf{c}, with S the overlap matrix. P.-O. Löwdin (1950) removed S with the symmetric orthogonalization |\psi\rangle = S^{-1/2}|\phi\rangle. Among all orthonormal sets, it is the one closest to the original orbitals, and it treats every orbital alike, so the new basis keeps the symmetry of the crystal. In that basis the problem is an ordinary eigenproblem for S^{-1/2}HS^{-1/2}, with the same bands. The cost is range: the orthogonalized orbitals have tails on the neighbours, so the hoppings between them reach further than those of H. An orthogonal tight-binding model with a few hoppings is a truncation of this exact one.">
