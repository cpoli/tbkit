Floquet
-------

Periodically driven lattices: quasienergies, the Floquet Hamiltonian,
dynamic localization and coherent destruction of tunnelling, the
photovoltaic Hall effect of graphene in circularly polarized light, a
Chern insulator engineered with that light, and anomalous Floquet
phases, whose edge states the winding number counts while every Chern
number vanishes.

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="In a static insulator the chiral edge states in a gap are counted by the Chern numbers of the bands below it. Kitagawa, Berg, Rudner and Demler (2010) found that a periodically driven lattice escapes this rule: since quasienergies are only defined modulo \omega = 2\pi/T, edge states can wind around the whole Floquet zone, and a driven system can have chiral edge states although every Floquet band has C = 0. Rudner, Lindner, Berg and Levin (2013) built the minimal example -- a five-step drive of the bipartite square lattice in which the hopping J is on along one bond direction \mathbf{b}_s per step (right, up, left, down, then a pause), all under a sublattice potential \delta_{AB} = \epsilon_A - \epsilon_B -- and the invariant that counts these states: the winding number">
