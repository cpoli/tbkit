Correlations
------------

Interacting electrons, in the Hartree-Fock mean-field approximation of
the Hubbard model: the magnetism of graphene's zigzag edges and of
triangular nanoislands, Lieb's theorem and the ferrimagnet of the Lieb
lattice, and flat-band ferromagnetism (Nagaoka, Mielke, Tasaki). The
non-collinear mean field finds the 120-degree order of the frustrated
triangular lattice.

A triangle with zigzag edges has all three edges on the same sublattice, A, and more A than B sites. A bipartite Hamiltonian then has (at least) N_Z = |A| - |B| states at exactly zero energy, all on sublattice A and concentrated on the edges. At half filling they hold N_Z electrons: like the open shell of an atom, they are filled with parallel spins (Hund&#x27;s rule), and the flake carries a spin S = N_Z/2 -- for any repulsion U, however small, while a hexagonal flake needs U above a threshold. This is Lieb&#x27;s theorem made concrete: the moment grows with the size of the triangle. Triangular nanographenes (the &quot;triangulenes&quot;) were synthesized and seen to be magnetic in 2017-2021.">
