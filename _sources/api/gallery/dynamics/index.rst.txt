Dynamics
--------

Real-time wavepacket propagation: Bloch oscillations and the
Wannier-Stark ladder under a uniform lattice tilt, Landau-Zener
tunnelling and Zener breakdown between bands, and light in waveguide
arrays (the directional coupler, discrete diffraction and diffraction
management).

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="A uniform force on an electron in a periodic lattice does not produce uniform acceleration, the way it would in free space: Bloch&#x27;s 1929 argument (formalized by Wannier in 1960) shows the electron instead oscillates periodically in real space, with period T_B=2\pi/F (in units where \hbar=1). In real crystals scattering happens on a far shorter timescale than T_B, which is why the effect went unobserved for over 60 years -- it took artificial semiconductor superlattices (Waschke et al., 1993), with a far larger effective lattice constant, to see it directly. See /history/driven_breakthroughs for the full historical context.">
