Flat Bands
----------

The kagome and Lieb lattices' exactly flat bands, the natural home for
strong-correlation physics via Lieb's theorem.

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Some lattice geometries host a perfectly flat (dispersionless) band with uniform nearest-neighbor hopping alone -- destructive interference confines an eigenstate to a small loop of sites (a single hexagon on kagome, a single &quot;plus sign&quot; of four sites on Lieb) with exactly zero amplitude everywhere else, for every Bloch momentum simultaneously. A flat band&#x27;s macroscopic degeneracy is the natural home for strong-correlation physics: Lieb&#x27;s theorem guarantees the half-filled Hubbard model on a bipartite lattice with an unequal number of sites per sublattice (as Lieb&#x27;s own lattice has, 2 vs 1) is a ferromagnet, for any nonzero repulsion U -- one of the very few rigorous, non-perturbative results in the many-body problem. tbkit.lattices provides both lattices ready-made.">
