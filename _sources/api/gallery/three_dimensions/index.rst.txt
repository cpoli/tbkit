Three dimensions
----------------

Lattices in 3D space: the Anderson metal-insulator transition, which
exists only in three dimensions, and Weyl semimetals, whose band
touchings (located, with their chiralities, by a Weyl point finder) are
monopoles of Berry curvature with Fermi-arc surface states.

The consequence: in one and two dimensions every state is localized by any disorder, but in three dimensions a genuine metal-insulator transition survives, at a critical disorder -- W_c \approx 16.5\,t for the Anderson model on the cubic lattice, with onsite energies uniform in [-W/2, W/2].">
