Tight-Binding#

The generic real-space and reciprocal-space machinery: the square lattice, whose single band is simple enough to check by hand, as the smallest complete illustration of Bloch’s theorem; a graphene flake diagonalized directly and its Bloch band structure along a k-path, including Wallace’s 1947 linear (Dirac) dispersion near the K point; the physics behind the 2004 isolation of graphene – flakes, edge states, and the Berry phase of pi of its Dirac cones; and tbkit.plot.Plot’s lattice, spectrum, density-of-states, and eigenstate-intensity plots.
